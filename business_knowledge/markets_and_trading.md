# Markets & Trading Knowledge (reference for JARVIS analysis)

Reference knowledge JARVIS draws on for trading/markets discussion. NOT advice; NOT a
guarantee of profit. JARVIS uses this to reason and explain — never to auto-execute trades.

## Asset Classes & How They Behave
- Equities (stocks): ownership; driven by earnings, growth, multiples, sentiment, rates.
- Fixed income (bonds): price moves inverse to yields; duration = rate sensitivity.
- FX: relative value between currencies; driven by rate differentials, flows, risk sentiment.
- Commodities: supply/demand, seasonality, USD strength, inventories.
- Crypto: 24/7, high volatility, liquidity-and-narrative driven, regime shifts fast.
- Derivatives (options/futures): leverage + defined or undefined risk; price via greeks.

## Core Valuation (equities)
- DCF: intrinsic value = sum of discounted future free cash flows. Sensitive to growth & discount rate.
- Multiples: P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/S, P/B, PEG — compare to peers & history, not in isolation.
- Quality signals: ROIC > WACC (creates value), durable margins, low debt, growing FCF, moat.
- Catalysts: earnings surprises, guidance, buybacks, M&A, product cycles, macro shifts.

## Options Greeks (risk dimensions)
- Delta: directional exposure (≈ probability ITM). Gamma: rate of delta change.
- Theta: time decay (sellers' friend, buyers' enemy). Vega: sensitivity to implied volatility.
- Core structures: covered call (income), protective put (insurance), vertical spreads
  (defined risk directional), iron condor (range/premium), calendar (term-structure/vol).
- Sell premium when IV is rich (high IV rank); buy when cheap. Never sell naked undefined risk.

## Technical Analysis (context, not crystal ball)
- Trend: higher-highs/higher-lows (up), lower-lows/lower-highs (down). Trade with it, not against.
- Support/resistance, moving averages (20/50/200), volume confirmation.
- Momentum: RSI (overbought/oversold), MACD (trend/momentum). Divergences warn of exhaustion.
- Volatility: ATR for stop placement & sizing; Bollinger Bands for range/expansion.
- TA gives probabilities and levels, not certainties. Combine with thesis & risk management.

## Quant & Systematic Concepts
- Edge = positive expectancy after costs. Expectancy = (winrate*avgWin) - (lossrate*avgLoss).
- Backtest pitfalls: overfitting, lookahead bias, survivorship bias, ignoring slippage/fees.
- Walk-forward & out-of-sample testing separate real edges from curve-fits.
- Risk metrics: Sharpe (return/vol), Sortino (downside vol), max drawdown, Calmar.
- Kelly criterion sizes by edge — but use fractional Kelly (¼–½) to survive variance.
- Mean-reversion vs momentum are regime-dependent; diversify uncorrelated edges.

## Market Structure & Macro
- Liquidity, bid/ask spread, and slippage matter more than headline price for real fills.
- Macro drivers: Fed funds rate, yield curve, CPI/inflation, employment, GDP, earnings season.
- Risk-on vs risk-off rotation moves correlations toward 1 in crises (diversification fails when needed most).
- OPEX, index rebalances, and quad-witching create predictable flow distortions.

## Behavioral Discipline (where most edge is lost)
- Loss aversion, recency bias, confirmation bias, overconfidence — name them to resist them.
- The plan is made before the position; emotions arrive after. Pre-commitment beats willpower.
- Process > outcome: a good trade can lose, a bad trade can win. Judge the decision, not the result.
