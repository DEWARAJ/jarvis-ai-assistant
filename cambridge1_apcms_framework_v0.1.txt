1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
-------------------
Goal: Design a cell membrane-based exhaust aftertreatment device that fits
directly onto a vehicle exhaust pipe and selectively captures CO2, NOx,
particulate matter (PM2.5/PM10), hydrocarbons (HC), and carbon monoxide (CO)
from combustion exhaust gases before they are released into the atmosphere.

Key Innovation: A layered bio-inspired semi-permeable membrane stack integrated
into a retrofit exhaust collar unit — no engine modification required.

Target Vehicles: Petrol (gasoline), diesel, and hybrid ICE vehicles.
Fitment: Universal exhaust pipe collar (50mm–120mm diameter range).

2. EXHAUST GAS CHARACTERISATION (Operating Environment)
========================================================

2.1 Exhaust Gas Composition (Typical ICE — Diesel)
----------------------------------------------------
  CO2         :  2% – 12%  by volume
  H2O (steam) :  2% – 12%  by volume
  NOx (NO+NO2):  200 – 1500 ppm
  CO          :  100 – 3000 ppm (higher in petrol)
  HC          :  50 – 500 ppm
  PM (soot)   :  10 – 200 mg/m³
  O2          :  2% – 18%  (excess air)
  N2          :  ~70%  (balance)
  SOx         :  <50 ppm (low-sulphur fuel)

2.2 Thermodynamic Parameters
-----------------------------
  Exhaust gas temperature at tailpipe exit:
    Petrol (cold start)  :  100°C – 200°C
    Petrol (warm, cruise):  300°C – 500°C
    Diesel (cruise)      :  180°C – 380°C
    Diesel (DPF outlet)  :  150°C – 250°C
    Peak (hard accel.)   :  Up to 900°C (pre-cat)

  Exhaust mass flow rate:
    Small car (1.2L)     :  20 – 60 kg/hr
    Mid-size (2.0L)      :  40 – 120 kg/hr
    Heavy diesel (3.0L+) :  80 – 300 kg/hr

  Exhaust volumetric flow:
    Approx. 0.01 – 0.08 m³/s at tailpipe (varies with RPM/load)

  Exhaust back-pressure tolerance:
    Safe operational limit: <10 kPa additional back-pressure
    Critical limit         : >15 kPa risks engine damage / ECU fault codes

  Gas velocity at tailpipe:
    2 – 20 m/s (idle to high RPM)

  Reynolds Number (exhaust pipe, D=60mm):
    Re = ρVD/μ ≈ 5,000 – 50,000 → Turbulent regime

3. MEMBRANE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
================================

3.1 Concept — Layered Membrane Stack
--------------------------------------
The APCMS uses a multi-layer membrane collar consisting of:

  LAYER 1 — PRE-FILTER (Mechanical)
    Material : Ceramic fibre mesh or sintered metal foam
    Function : Removes coarse PM, soot, and condensed hydrocarbons
    Temp rating: up to 900°C
    Pore size  : 10–50 μm

  LAYER 2 — OXIDATION CATALYST LAYER
    Material : Platinum/Palladium washcoat on alumina substrate
    Function : Oxidises CO → CO2, HC → CO2 + H2O
    Operating temp: 200°C – 600°C
    Note     : This pre-conditions the gas for membrane selectivity

  LAYER 3 — NOx SELECTIVE MEMBRANE
    Material : Mixed ionic-electronic conducting (MIEC) perovskite membrane
               e.g. La0.6Sr0.4Co0.2Fe0.8O3-δ (LSCF) or BaZrO3-based
    Function : Selective permeation of NO and NO2 via oxygen vacancy diffusion
    Mechanism: NOx adsorbs on surface → dissociates → N2 released, O²⁻ conducted
    Operating temp: 400°C – 800°C (self-sustaining with exhaust heat)
    Selectivity: NOx/N2 selectivity factor ~50–200

  LAYER 4 — CO2 SELECTIVE MEMBRANE
    Material : Molten carbonate membrane (Li2CO3/Na2CO3 eutectic in porous
               ceramic support — alumina or YSZ)
    Mechanism: CO2 + O²⁻ → CO3²⁻ (permeates through melt) → CO2 released
               on permeate side for capture/storage
    Operating temp: 500°C – 700°C
    CO2/N2 selectivity: 100 – 1000+ (literature values)
    CO2 flux         : 1–10 mL/cm²/min at operating temperature

  LAYER 5 — FINE PARTICULATE FILTER
    Material : Cordierite or SiC honeycomb (similar to DPF)
    Function : Captures PM2.5 and ultrafine particles
    Regeneration: Passive (via exhaust heat) or active (resistive element)

  LAYER 6 — THERMAL MANAGEMENT JACKET
    Material : Stainless steel outer shell with ceramic fibre insulation
    Function : Maintains membrane operating temperature window
               Prevents outer surface burns (surface <60°C target)


3.2 Cross-Section Schematic (text representation)
---------------------------------------------------

  [EXHAUST GAS IN] →
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  L1: Ceramic Pre-filter (PM/soot)               │
  │  L2: Oxidation Catalyst (CO, HC removal)        │
  │  L3: MIEC Perovskite NOx Membrane               │
  │  L4: Molten Carbonate CO2 Membrane              │
  │  L5: SiC Fine Particulate Filter                │
  │  L6: Thermal Jacket + Insulation                │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  → [CLEANED GAS OUT — primarily N2, H2O, residual CO2]

  Permeate Side (captured gases routed to):
    NOx → Catalytic reduction chamber → N2 vented safely
    CO2 → Compressed storage canister (exchangeable)
         OR mineralisation cartridge (CO2 + Ca(OH)2 → CaCO3)

4. THERMODYNAMIC ANALYSIS
==========================

4.1 Energy Balance
-------------------
  Exhaust enthalpy flux (Q_exhaust):
    Q = ṁ × Cp × ΔT
    ṁ = 0.03 kg/s (mid-size petrol, cruise)
    Cp ≈ 1.05 kJ/kg·K (exhaust gas mixture)
    ΔT = 400 – 25 = 375 K
    Q ≈ 0.03 × 1.05 × 375 ≈ 11.8 kW available thermal energy

  Cold start gap: below 300°C, membranes inactive — resistive pre-heater
                  (200W electric element) bridges cold-start gap (~90 sec)

4.2 Back-Pressure Analysis
----------------------------
  ΔP across membrane stack (estimated):
    L1 (ceramic mesh)      : 0.5 – 1.0 kPa
    L2 (catalyst washcoat) : 1.0 – 2.0 kPa
    L3 (MIEC membrane)     : 1.5 – 3.0 kPa
    L4 (molten carbonate)  : 2.0 – 4.0 kPa
    L5 (SiC filter)        : 0.5 – 1.5 kPa
    TOTAL estimated         : 5.5 – 11.5 kPa

  Mitigation: Annular membrane design to increase face area and reduce ΔP < 8 kPa.

4.3 Fluid Dynamics — Airflow Modelling
----------------------------------------
  Continuity : ∇·(ρV) = 0
  Momentum   : ρ(V·∇)V = -∇P + μ∇²V
  Energy     : ρCp(V·∇T) = k∇²T + Q_reaction

  Turbulence model: k-ε or k-ω SST (OpenFOAM / ANSYS Fluent)
  Target membrane face velocity: 0.1 – 0.5 m/s
  Required membrane face area: ~0.10 m² (annular/radial arrangement)

5. MECHANICAL & FITMENT DESIGN
================================

  Housing  : Grade 316L stainless steel
  Insulation: Aerogel blanket + ceramic fibre wrap
  OD       : 150 – 250 mm | Length: 300 – 500 mm | Weight: <4 kg
  Fitment  : V-band clamp (50–120mm ID universal adapters)
  Sealing  : High-temp graphite gaskets

  CO2 Canister: 2L replaceable pressure vessel (max 5 bar), ~500g CO2
  Exchange interval: ~300–500 km | Option B: Ca(OH)2 mineralisation cartridge

  Sensors  : Type K thermocouple, differential pressure, NDIR CO2, NOx
  Control  : STM32 MCU, BLE smartphone interface
  Power    : 12V vehicle feed, <5A nominal

9. OPEN TECHNICAL CHALLENGES

  2. Cold-start performance gap (<300°C)
  3. Back-pressure management via annular geometry
  4. CO2 canister exchange logistics

  6. Perovskite stability in high-H2O exhaust
  7. Unit cost target: <£200 at volume
