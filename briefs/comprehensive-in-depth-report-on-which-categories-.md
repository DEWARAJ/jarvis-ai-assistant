Communication and collaboration are still foundational and likely to remain so for years. Messaging and video tools such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, iMessage, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet are essential because they are the default layer for personal, work, and group coordination; their importance will probably rise further as more work becomes remote, distributed, and asynchronous.
